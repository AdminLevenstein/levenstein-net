7. Roxie's food

  • Weigh it: 280 grams a day, total. Use the kitchen scale. No extra.
  • New brand: Pro Plan Large Adult. Order on a Monday when about 3 kg of Royal Canin is left. Max Rp 80.000 per kg. Shopee Mall / official store only — never repack (curah).
  • Change slowly over 10 days:
    • Days 1–3: ¾ Royal Canin + ¼ Pro Plan
    • Days 4–6: ½ + ½
    • Days 7–9: ¼ Royal Canin + ¾ Pro Plan
    • Day 10: all Pro Plan
  • If Roxie has an upset stomach or itchy skin, go back to Royal Canin 10 kg (Murah Pet Shop, max Rp 860.000) and report it.
  • Pour all dog food into the locking plastic bin the day it arrives (rats).

8. Rules

  • Max price: if it costs more than the max, don't buy it — buy it next week or report it.
  • Pasar receipts: write the price in your nota book and ask the seller to sign, or take a photo of the item with the price.
  • Online: Official Store, Shopee Mall or Star+ sellers only. Screenshot every order.
  • Cold items: always GoSend / Grab Instant. Never cargo.
  • Heavy items (beer, cans, dog food): online delivery — never on the motorbike.
  • Rats: bread, coffee, onions go into the sealed boxes as soon as you get home.
  • Change: bring back the cash change — don't roll it into the next purchase.
