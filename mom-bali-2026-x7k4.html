1. At a glance

  • Dates: Mom lands Thu 26 Nov at 16:15 and leaves Thu 10 Dec at 17:20. That is 13 full days.
  • Shape of the plan: on weekdays Mom has a spa or jewelry outing with a driver while you work 10:00 to 18:00, and you join from about 18:15. Weekends are beach and family time with Max.
  • Spas: COMO Shambhala (Canggu), La Réserve Secret Spa (Pererenan), Spa Alila or AWAY Spa (Seminyak), Sacred River Spa at Four Seasons Sayan (Ubud), Shankha Spa at Hyatt Regency (Sanur), and The Bvlgari Spa (Uluwatu) as the big-ticket optional one.
  • Four jewelry outings: Jemme (Seminyak, also a restaurant), John Hardy and Tulola (Seminyak), the John Hardy workshop tour near Ubud, and Atlas Pearls plus Gardens of the Sun (Sanur).
  • La Réserve (Zoho task due 25 Nov): I put Mom's treatment on Mon 30 Nov at 16:00, with you arriving at 18:15 to look around and have dinner there. See the catch in section 6 before you book.
  • Nothing is booked. Section 6 lists what needs booking and by when.

2. What I planned around

  • Your work: weekdays 10:00 to 18:00, with the 10:00 to 11:00 product-development block at your desk. Morning workout kept; BJJ on Saturday mornings.
  • Max: with you Thu noon to Sun 17:00, so Thu to Sun evenings are family dinners at home or somewhere child-friendly, and Mon to Wed evenings are the adult dinners.
  • Already in your calendar: Sean's birthday at Mari Beach Club (Fri 27 Nov, 14:00 to 19:00), the call with Hunter (Mon 30 Nov, 16:30 to 17:00), Golden Hour Club at COMO Beach Club (Wed 2 Dec, guests from 18:00), Safira's 4th birthday (Sun 6 Dec, 10:00 to 13:00).
  • Thanksgiving: Thu 26 Nov is Thanksgiving and Mom's arrival day. Your email to her says Wanda and others want to plan it.
  • Jet lag: Chicago is 14 hours behind Bali, so the first three days are light and close to home.
  • Rainy season: late November and December usually bring afternoon downpours, so beach time is in the morning or at sunset and spa time is in the afternoon.
  • Assumption: Mom stays with you at the villa in Canggu and uses a car and driver for the day trips. Drive times below are my estimates from Canggu, not measured.

3. Day by day

Arrival and first weekend

Thu 26 Nov Arrival · Thanksgiving

  • 16:15 Mom lands at DPS. Pick-up is in your calendar. Home around 18:00.
  • Max is with you from noon.
  • Thanksgiving dinner: keep it early (about 19:00) and short. She will have been travelling for more than a day.

Fri 27 Nov Recovery day

  • Slow morning at the villa.
  • 11:30 COMO Shambhala Retreat at COMO Uma Canggu: a 60 or 90 minute massage, then lunch at the hotel. It is 10 to 15 minutes from the villa and a place you know.
  • 14:00 to 19:00 you are at Sean's birthday at Mari Beach Club. Mom can come along for an hour of beach and sunset with you and Max, or nap at home.

Sat 28 Nov Beach, all together

  • Morning: your BJJ. Mom has breakfast and a swim at the villa.
  • 16:00 Beach all together (you, Mom, Max) for a walk and sunset, then an early dinner nearby.

Sun 29 Nov Beach morning Jewelry + dinner

  • 08:00 Beach morning all together, before the heat and the rain.
  • 17:00 Max goes back to Wanda.
  • 18:30 Jemme, Jl Petitenget 28, Seminyak: a fine-jewelry showroom (diamonds and coloured stones) with its own restaurant, open 11:00 to 23:00. Browse first, then dinner at the same address. This is the one jewelry stop you can do together without leaving work.

Week one

Mon 30 Nov La Réserve

  • Free morning.
  • 16:00 La Réserve 1785 Secret Spa, Pererenan (about 15 to 20 minutes). Suggested: Ayurvedic Shirodhara, 90 minutes, or the Secret Spa Signature Massage, 60 minutes. The spa closes at 19:00.
  • 16:30 to 17:00 your call with Hunter.
  • 18:15 You arrive, look around the property for future Golden Hour evenings, then dinner there with Mom. The hotel is listed as adults-only, which is why this is on a Monday and not on a Max day.

Tue 1 Dec Seminyak jewelry day

  • 11:30 John Hardy Seminyak Boutique and Gallery, Jl Petitenget (30 to 45 minutes). Lunch at its Long Table or Kitchen restaurant on site.
  • 14:00 Tulola, Jl Raya Seminyak: Indonesian-inspired gold, pearl and gemstone pieces. Open 11:00 to 20:00.
  • 15:30 Spa Alila Seminyak (oceanfront) or AWAY Spa at W Bali, both near Petitenget.
  • 18:30 You join for a sunset dinner in Seminyak.

Wed 2 Dec Golden Hour Club night

  • Quiet day at the villa.
  • 15:30 Optional: a second COMO Shambhala treatment (a facial this time). It is at the same resort as the evening's venue, so she is already there.
  • 18:00 to 20:30 Golden Hour Club at COMO Beach Club. Your call whether Mom comes as your guest. Laure from La Réserve has said she will be there.

Thu 3 Dec Ubud: John Hardy workshop + Four Seasons

  • 09:00 Leave Canggu with a driver (60 to 75 minutes).
  • 10:30 John Hardy Kapal Bamboo Boutique and Artisan Workshop, Mambal: guided workshop tour to watch the pieces being made, then the boutique. Tours run Monday to Friday only and must be reserved a day ahead.
  • 13:00 Lunch at Four Seasons Sayan (about 20 minutes from Mambal).
  • 14:30 Sacred River Spa, Four Seasons Resort Bali at Sayan: riverside treatment.
  • 18:30 Home. Max is with you from noon, so dinner at the villa.

Fri 4 Dec Rest day

  • Nothing fixed. A mid-trip day off after Ubud.
  • Optional: Regent Bali Canggu spa (warm quartz-sand-bed massage, vitality pool, saunas), 10 to 15 minutes away. Whether they take day guests is not confirmed.
  • Optional: a casual browse in Canggu at LeeLoo Bird (Jl Pantai Pererenan 107) or Luna & Rose (Echo Beach). These are design-led silver shops, not fine jewelry.

Second weekend

Sat 5 Dec Beach, all together

  • Morning: your BJJ.
  • 15:30 Beach and sunset all together, early dinner out with Max.

Sun 6 Dec Family

  • 10:00 to 13:00 Safira's 4th birthday at Narawangsa Villas, Kerobokan (you and Max). Mom can join or have the morning to herself. RSVP is due Sun 29 Nov with the number of adults and children.
  • Afternoon at the villa pool. 17:00 Max goes back to Wanda.
  • Evening: dinner for two, your choice.

Week two

Mon 7 Dec Uluwatu and Jimbaran (optional big day)

  • 11:00 Leave Canggu (75 to 100 minutes; this is the longest drive of the trip).
  • 13:00 The Bvlgari Spa, Bvlgari Resort Bali, Uluwatu: the most lavish spa on the list, in an antique Javanese house with a cliff-edge pavilion.
  • 16:00 John Hardy Boutique at AYANA Resort, Jimbaran (open 12:00 to 21:00), on the way back. AYANA also has the large Aquatonic heated seawater pool if she wants a soak (open 10:00 to 19:00).
  • 18:30 If you can leave at 17:00, meet her for a sunset seafood dinner in Jimbaran. Otherwise she is home by about 19:30.
  • If this is too much driving, drop it and repeat her favourite nearby spa instead.

Tue 8 Dec Sanur: pearls and diamonds

  • 10:00 Leave Canggu (50 to 70 minutes).
  • 11:15 Atlas Pearls showroom, Jl Bypass Ngurah Rai 49, Sanur: South Sea pearls from the company's own farms.
  • 12:30 Gardens of the Sun, Jl Kanda 15B, Sanur: diamonds and coloured gemstones, custom work. Open Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 16:00 only.
  • 14:30 Shankha Spa, Hyatt Regency Bali, Sanur: spa villas, hydrotherapy pools and saunas.
  • 18:30 Home. Adult dinner in Canggu.

Wed 9 Dec Last full day

  • Morning: collect anything that was resized or made to order during the trip.
  • 15:00 Farewell treatment at whichever spa she liked best (La Réserve and COMO are the two closest).
  • 19:00 Farewell dinner. No Max that night, so it can be a proper one.

Thu 10 Dec Departure

  • Morning to pack. Max arrives at noon and can say goodbye.
  • 13:15 Leave for the airport. Check-in block starts 14:20; flight departs 17:20.
