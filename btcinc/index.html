2. The idea

  • What: one evening in Bali, built on your own Bitcoin for Corporations format in miniature: short panels, a sunset cocktail hour, then a VIP dinner.
  • When: the second half of May 2027, about three months before Hong Kong (26–27 August 2027) — and inside the six to eight months' notice you asked for.
  • Where: the Grand Hyatt Bali or a venue of the same standard.
  • Who: 60 to 100 guests, by invitation only: family-office principals, founders, fund managers and long-term bitcoin holders from Bali, Jakarta, Singapore and the region.
  • Purpose: one. Every guest leaves holding a Whale Pass to Hong Kong, or wanting one.
  • Whose event: yours. It carries the Bitcoin Hong Kong and Bitcoin Magazine name. We are the local producer and host.

5. The risk sits with me

  1. You say yes in principle. Cost to you: nothing.
  2. You switch on the Bali code; we open the invitation list. Passes start selling before anything is booked.
  3. When Whale Pass sign-ups reach break-even, I front the money to reserve the Grand Hyatt or a comparable venue. My cash, not yours.
  4. Event costs are settled out of pass revenue the event has already produced, up to $15,000. Not one dollar above the cap is spent without your written approval.
  5. If sign-ups do not reach the line by a date we agree, we call it off. You have spent nothing, nothing was announced that cannot be quietly withdrawn, and you keep every pass that was sold.
Put plainly: the worst case for BTC Inc is a handful of extra Whale Passes sold and no event. The expected case is fifteen or more, a full room, and a format you can repeat.

6. Why Bali, and why us

  • The idea: Bali is where a good part of Asia's founders and crypto wealth comes to live or to switch off. Singapore, Jakarta, Kuala Lumpur and Taipei are a short flight away, and to those people Bali and Hong Kong are the same trip. Premium hotel space here costs a fraction of Hong Kong or Singapore.
  • The host: I have traded bitcoin derivatives for seven years and I live here. I curate the Bali Golden Hour Club, a private founders' community, so the room is one I already host. I am not selling these guests anything that night. The only product in the room is Hong Kong.
  • The company: we are a local team with a local entity. We handle the permits properly, and we have produced a hotel event in Bali at this budget before.

7. What it opens up next

The Warm-Up is the first step, not the whole staircase. Each of these can be added only if the step before it worked.

  • Before — Bitcoin Magazine salons at Golden Hour (Q1 2027): one or two evenings of 30 to 50 people, a talk and a dinner, filmed for your channels. They build the list the Warm-Up sells to.
  • Before — content: a regular Bitcoin Magazine column or video segment from me on bitcoin derivatives, such as what the futures basis is saying against T-bills this month. Market commentary, and a steady drumbeat toward May.
  • During — a panel for this region: bitcoin income and Sharia compliance, led by a qualified scholar. It matters in Indonesia and Malaysia, and it travels straight to your MENA event in Abu Dhabi.
  • After — Hong Kong: I would be glad to speak at the BFC session on treasury hedging and earning yield on balance-sheet bitcoin with derivatives, which is what your corporate members are asking about.
  • After — Hong Kong to Bali: a two-day, invitation-only retreat the week after Hong Kong for 50 to 80 Whale Pass holders, sold as a Whale add-on. Same model: we produce, you sell.
  • Ongoing — community partner: make us your Indonesia and Southeast Asia partner for Hong Kong 2027 and Tokyo 2028 on your standard referral terms. You pay only on results.
  • 2028 — a full Bitcoin for Corporations Bali Session: the 200-person format on your calendar, once Bali has proved itself with real numbers.

8. Questions you would be right to ask

  • "We do not have the people." You do not need any. Your side is a ticketing code, one email and an approval.
  • "What if nobody buys?" Then nothing is booked and nothing is spent. The trigger is sales, not hope.
  • "What about our brand?" You approve the guest list, the run of show and anything that carries your logo. Nothing goes out without your sign-off.
  • "Who carries the cash risk?" I do. The venue deposit is mine to front.
  • "Why decide now?" Because of your own rule. Six months before late May is this month.
  • "What do you get out of it?" A good room in my home town, and a working relationship with the people who run the best conference in Bitcoin. I would rather earn that than buy it.
